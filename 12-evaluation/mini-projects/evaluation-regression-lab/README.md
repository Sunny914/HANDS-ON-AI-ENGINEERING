# AI Evaluation and Regression Lab

A small lab that scores a fixture system on two different questions, such as retrieval quality and groundedness, or tool correctness and task completion. It stores a baseline, compares a second configuration, and writes a regression report.

This is the evaluation harness the roadmap pointed at. The new work is versioned cases, separate metrics, a gate, and an honest limit on what a small sample shows. You may point the runner at predictions from an earlier stage, or at hand-written prediction files. You do not have to rebuild that stage.
